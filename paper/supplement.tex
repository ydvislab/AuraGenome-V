\texttt{results/a1} in the code repository. Spearman correlations are between predicted and measured effects of

types averaged). Siraj-derived tables are released under CC-BY-NC 4.0. Table~S6 has two parts (S6a, S6b). Table~S15 is copied from the result files named in its source.
